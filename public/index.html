Our Services

Donate medicine and equipment

List medicines, mobility aids, or medical equipment you no longer need. Donors set the item, condition, and pickup or drop-off details in minutes.

Find equipment, free or low-cost

Search available items from verified donors near you. Take what's offered for free, or purchase equipment directly when a donor has set a price.

Request through a registered NGO

Search registered NGOs near you and request medicine they've collected from donors. NGOs verify and distribute items directly to people who need them.

Partner as an NGO

Register your organization to receive donated medicine from donors and distribute it to people in your community who request it.
